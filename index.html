For the girl who became my favourite part of these six months.

Ananta,

Six months sounds like just a number when you say it out loud. But when I actually stop and think about it, it feels like so much more than that.

Somehow, six months became a whole little universe of its own — one that has you somewhere in almost every corner of it.

And honestly, I don't think I ever realised when you started becoming such an important part of my everyday life.

It wasn't one huge moment.

I think it happened in hundreds of tiny ones.

In the random conversations. The stupid jokes. The times we had absolutely nothing to talk about and somehow still didn't want the conversation to end.

In the moments when seeing your name on my screen could instantly make an ordinary day feel a little better.

In all those small things that probably didn't seem important at the time, but somehow became the things I remember the most.

You became someone I could look forward to. Someone whose presence could change my mood without even trying.

And somewhere between all of those little moments, you became something I never expected you to become.

You became home.

I know we're not perfect. We have our weird days, our misunderstandings, our overthinking, our unnecessary arguments and those conversations where one of us says “I'm fine” when we both know that's definitely not the complete story. 😭

But maybe that's also what makes this ours.

It isn't some perfectly written story where everything goes exactly right.

It's two people learning each other, figuring things out, laughing at completely stupid things, getting annoyed, forgiving, understanding and somehow continuing to choose each other.

And that's what I love about us.

I don't need every day to be perfect. I just want the ordinary days to still have you somewhere in them.

Because you have this strange ability to make normal things feel special without even trying.

A conversation becomes something I'll remember. A random joke becomes an inside thing. A normal day suddenly becomes one I'm glad happened.

And maybe you don't always realise how much the little things matter to me.

But they do.

More than you probably know.

Six months later, I still look at everything we've become and think about how unexpectedly beautiful it is that I found you.

I don't know exactly what the next six months will look like.

I don't know what every chapter after this one will bring.

But I know one thing.

I want to keep writing them with you.

So here's to six months of us.

To everything we've learned. To all the laughter. To all the ridiculous conversations. To all the little things that somehow became big things.

To the days that were easy, and the days that weren't.

And to all the days we haven't reached yet.

Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life.

Thank you for being you.

And thank you for making these six months feel like something I never want to forget.

Happy six months, Ananta.

If I had to start these six months all over again, knowing everything I know now...

I'd still find you.

— Varun ♡
